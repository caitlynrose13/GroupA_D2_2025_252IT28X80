📅 Month 7: Physical Security and Data Privacy

9. True or False: Physical security measures are solely concerned with protecting equipment from external physical threats, such as theft.
Answer: False ✅
Explanation (verbatim):

Physical security goes beyond just preventing external theft of equipment. It also encompasses a wider range of measures, including safeguarding secure areas within buildings, implementing strict physical entry controls, securing offices and facilities, and protecting against environmental threats like fire or water damage.

10. True or False: Data privacy is mainly concerned with using strong passwords and encryption to stop hackers from accessing sensitive information.
Answer: False ✅
Explanation (verbatim):

While strong passwords and encryption are important cybersecurity measures to protect data, data privacy is a broader concept. It focuses on the appropriate use and overall management of data throughout its entire lifecycle, from how it is collected and processed to how it is stored, shared, and eventually disposed of.

11. True or False: Data privacy within an organisation is solely about the technical process of encrypting data to prevent unauthorized access.
Answer: False ✅
Explanation (verbatim):

Data privacy is a broader concept than just technical encryption. While encryption is an important tool for protecting data confidentiality, privacy is fundamentally about the appropriate use and management of data throughout its entire lifecycle. This includes how data is collected, used, shared, retained, and disposed of, encompassing organisational policies, procedures, and the behaviour of all individuals handling data, not just technical safeguards.

12. True or False: Physical security in an organisation is only about keeping unauthorised people out of computer rooms.
Answer: False ✅
Explanation (verbatim):

Physical security involves much more than just restricting entry to computer rooms. It encompasses a wide range of protections, including safeguarding all secure areas, controlling physical access to facilities, and protecting against environmental threats like fire or floods.
