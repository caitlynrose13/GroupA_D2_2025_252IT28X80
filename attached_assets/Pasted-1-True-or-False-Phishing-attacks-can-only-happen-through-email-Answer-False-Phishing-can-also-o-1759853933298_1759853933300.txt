1. True or False: Phishing attacks can only happen through email.
Answer: False

Phishing can also occur via SMS (smishing), phone calls (vishing), or fake websites.

2. Which of the following is a common red flag of a phishing email?

a) Proper grammar and spelling

b) Urgent requests for action

c) Sent from a trusted company domain

d) Personalized signature

Answer: b) Urgent requests for action

Phishing emails often try to create a sense of urgency to trick you into acting quickly without thinking.

3. True or False: Social engineering relies more on manipulating people than exploiting technical vulnerabilities.
Answer: True

Social engineering attacks involve tricking or manipulating individuals to gain sensitive information or access.

4. The safest step when you suspect phishing is:

a) Click the link to confirm authenticity

b) Forward it to friends for advice

c) Report it to IT/security immediately

d) Reply asking for more details

Answer: c) Report it to IT/security immediately

If you suspect a phishing attempt, reporting it promptly to IT or security personnel is the safest step.

Month 2: Password Management and Authentication

5. True/False: “Summer2025” is considered a strong password.
Answer: False

It’s predictable and based on common patterns (e.g., a word + year), making it easy for attackers to guess.

6. What does Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) require?

a) Only a username and password

b) Two or more independent verification methods

c) A long password only

d) A password hint

Answer: b) Two or more independent verification methods

MFA requires something you know (password), something you have (e.g., phone for a code), or something you are (biometrics).

7. What is the main risk of reusing passwords across accounts?

a) Accounts load faster

b) Reduced chance of forgetting passwords

c) If one account is hacked, others can be compromised

d) No real risk exists

Answer: c) If one account is hacked, others can be compromised

Reusing passwords means a breach of one account could potentially lead to the compromise of all your accounts using the same password.

8. Which of the following is the strongest password?

a) Winter2025

b) johndoe!

c) !C0ffeeMug#92$

d) password123

Answer: c) !C0ffeeMug#92$

A strong password should include a mix of upper and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters, making it hard to guess.

Month 3: Malware and Ransomware

9. What is the best response to a ransomware attack?

a) Pay the ransom quickly

b) Disconnect from the network and call IT/security

c) Restart the computer

d) Try to guess the password

Answer: b) Disconnect from the network and call IT/security

The first step is to isolate the affected system to prevent the malware from spreading, then contact IT/security.

10. True/False: Malware can only infect a device through email attachments.
Answer: False

Malware can also spread via USB drives, software downloads, malicious websites, and even network vulnerabilities.

11. Which of the following can spread malware?

a) Suspicious email attachments

b) Downloading unverified software

c) Visiting unsafe websites

d) All of the above

Answer: d) All of the above

Malware can be spread through multiple vectors, including suspicious email attachments, unsafe websites, and unverified software downloads.

12. True/False: Ransomware locks or encrypts your files and demands payment for release.
Answer: True

Ransomware is specifically designed to lock or encrypt files and demand a ransom payment for their release.
